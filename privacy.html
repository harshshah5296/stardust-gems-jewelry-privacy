Privacy Policy

STARDUST Trend Research · Updated 13 October 2026

STARDUST GEMS & JEWELRY is developing STARDUST Trend Research, a private jewelry product planning tool. Contact us at stardustgnj@gmail.com with questions or requests about this policy.

Current status

The tool is currently running locally for its owner. Its Pinterest API connection has not been approved or activated. It does not currently collect Pinterest account data through that API. This public page does not ask visitors to sign in or submit personal information.

Data the tool may use after authorization

If Pinterest approves the requested access and an account holder authorizes the connection, the tool may read permitted Pins, Boards, links, descriptions, creator attribution, dates and available analytics. It may also store source links, measurement notes and limited reference metadata entered by the owner. The exact data available depends on Pinterest's approved permissions; approval to read other users' data is a separate decision.

Why we use it

We use permitted information to identify jewelry themes, compare demand signals, document sources and plan original product concepts. We do not use Pinterest account data to copy another creator's distinctive design or to publish Pins. We do not sell Pinterest account data.

Storage and sharing

Research records and generated concepts are stored on the owner's computer in the local STARDUST application. The owner can view and manage them there. We do not provide those records to advertisers or data brokers. The service that hosts this public page may receive ordinary technical requests needed to deliver it. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to this page.

Retention and requests

Research records are kept until the owner removes them or closes the project, subject to any platform rules that require earlier deletion. Some dated collection audit records may remain until removed from local storage. To ask about access, correction or deletion of data associated with you, email stardustgnj@gmail.com. We will review and respond in line with applicable requirements.

Changes

We will update this page when the tool's data practices change. The update date above shows the latest version.
